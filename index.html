Good to know

Questions before your first taster?

Do I need experience?

No. All six tasters are designed as introductions for beginners.

Will I receive a professional qualification?

No. These are 90-minute discovery sessions, not accredited trade training or certifications.

How much does a session cost?

Proposed pilot prices range from €39 to €59, depending on the activity and equipment.

What if I'm worried about the physical demands?

A tradesperson will explain the working conditions and practical demands of the occupation. Ask the organiser about accessibility and activity requirements before attending.

Can I pursue the career afterwards?

Yes, you can explore relevant apprenticeship, FET or specialist training pathways. Entry requirements vary, and attending a taster does not guarantee admission or employment.
